# Quick Testing Guide (5-Minute Walkthrough)

Here is a quick step-by-step guide to test all key features using two accounts on your phone or between two phones:

---

### Step 1: Create Two Test Accounts
1. **Hospital Account:**
   - Tap **Register** ➡️ select **Hospital / Blood Bank**.
   - Name: `Aldel Hospital Palghar`, Email: `hospital@test.com`, Password: `password123`.
2. **Donor Account:**
   - Sign out ➡️ tap **Register** ➡️ select **Individual Donor**.
   - Name: `Rahul Patil`, Blood Group: `O+`, City: `Palghar`, Email: `donor@test.com`, Password: `password123`.

---

### Step 2: Hospital Broadcasts an SOS
1. Log in as **Hospital** (`hospital@test.com`).
2. Tap **`BROADCAST HOSPITAL SOS REQUEST`** (or the red **+** button).
3. Select **O+**, enter units (`2`), choose **Critical**, and tap **`Publish SOS Broadcast`**.
4. You will see it appear under **"Your Active SOS Broadcasts"** with a **"Manage Queue"** button.

---

### Step 3: Donor Applies to Donate
1. Log in as **Donor** (`donor@test.com`).
2. Go to the **Requests** tab (or check the Home screen).
3. You will see Aldel Hospital's SOS request with a red urgency badge.
4. Tap **`Respond`** ➡️ review case ➡️ tap **`Volunteer to Donate`**.

---

### Step 4: Hospital Selects the Donor
1. Switch back to **Hospital** account.
2. Tap **`Manage Queue`** on the SOS request.
3. You will see `Rahul Patil (O+)` in the **Volunteer Donor Queue**.
4. Tap **`Select Donor`** ➡️ tap **`Confirm Selection`**.
   - *The SOS immediately disappears from all other donors' dashboards!*

---

### Step 5: Donor Sees Acceptance & Contacts Hospital
1. Switch back to **Donor** account.
2. Notice the green **"YOU ARE THE ACCEPTED DONOR!"** banner at the top of Home and Requests screens:
   - 📞 Tap **`Call Hospital`** ➡️ opens phone dialer with hospital desk number.
   - 🧭 Tap **`Directions`** ➡️ opens Google Maps route to the hospital.

---

### Step 6: Hospital Fulfills the Request
1. On the **Hospital** account, tap **`Mark as Fulfilled`** ➡️ **`Confirm`**.
2. Switch to **Donor** account:
   - Banner changes to **FULFILLED**.
   - Donor receives a **"Donation Fulfilled!"** notification.
   - In **Profile**, `My Donations` and `Lives Saved` both increment by **+1**.

---

### Step 7: Test Palghar Hospitals Directory
1. As a donor, tap the **Hospitals** tab in the bottom bar.
2. Scroll through the 20 Palghar District facilities (Dahanu, Jawhar, Wada, Boisar, Manor, etc.).
3. Test **Call** (dials casualty desk) and **Directions** (opens Google Maps).
